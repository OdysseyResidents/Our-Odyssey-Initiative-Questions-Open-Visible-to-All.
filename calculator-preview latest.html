Full disclaimer

This calculator gives estimates only, based on Our Odyssey Initiative documents and spreadsheets as of October 2026. It was made by residents for the Our Odyssey Initiative and is not produced or endorsed by Villa Vie Residences. It is not a bill, an offer to sell, a solicitation, a contract, or legal, tax, or financial advice, and it does not change or assess any existing contract you hold. Please don't send money based on this page. Final repurchase fees, monthly fees, and program terms will be set by the elected Resident Board of Directors and may differ from what you see here. The founders' answers reflect current thinking and may change as new information comes in. Figures marked Likely, Placeholder, Pending, or Under review are not final. Monthly fees shown are today's figures and do not include future increases. Fees may be adjusted over time, including yearly adjustments for inflation or the cost of living (such as changes in the Consumer Price Index), and changes in costs the ship can't control, such as fuel, port and canal fees, insurance, crew wages, food and supplies, currency exchange rates, maintenance, dry dock work, new regulations, and contributions to the capital reserve fund. Additional assessments approved by the Resident Board are also possible if unexpected costs arise. The first year estimate does not include taxes, travel to and from the ship, personal insurance, or personal spending not covered by the monthly fee. The initiative takes effect only if the Critical Mass Threshold is met. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. For your personal situation, please ask on the Q&A page or contact the founders directly, and consider speaking with your own legal or financial adviser before committing funds.

Sources: Our Odyssey resident letter and Cabin Repurchase Calculation (October 2026), the founders' master spreadsheet, the founders' answers of October 6, 2026, and the Q&A page.
